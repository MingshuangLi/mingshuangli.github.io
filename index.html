About

Clinician-scientist and Au.D. educator

Dr. Li is a tenure-track assistant professor and dispensing audiologist in the Department of Communication Disorders and Sciences at CSUN. He serves as Supervising Faculty of the CSUN Hearing Science Laboratory and previously served as Interim Director of the Doctor of Audiology program.

His research examines speech perception and enhancement in noise, with a growing emphasis on auditory brainstem responses to complex sounds and frequency-following responses. He also teaches and clinically supervises students across hearing science, electrophysiology, advanced audiometry, pediatric audiology, and research methods.

Research

Behavioral, neural, and translational approaches

Primary program

Speech enhancement

Developing and evaluating formant-based enhancement strategies for speech affected by noise, reduced signal-to-noise ratio, and source-related muffling.

Growing program

FFR and cABR

Investigating neural encoding of complex sounds, including effects of speech content and spectral and temporal modulation.

Foundation

Speech perception

Studying language experience, category mapping, psychoacoustic sensitivity, masking release, pitch processing, and speech perception in noise.

Program vision

How can behavioral, neural, and signal-processing approaches improve speech perception under degraded listening conditions?

Dissociating category mapping and formant-cue sensitivity in second-language vowel perception and masking release in noise

Mingshuang Li, Can Xu, Chang Liu

引用信息

Li, M.*, Xu, C., & Liu, C. Dissociating category mapping and formant-cue sensitivity in second-language vowel perception and masking release in noise. Proceedings of Meetings on Acoustics, 61(1), 060001.

DOI: 10.1121/2.0002315

研究概述

本研究在安静、稳态噪声和时间调制噪声中，比较元音识别、言语及非言语 F2 辨别，借助任务间的表现差异区分第二语言类别映射与基本共振峰线索敏感度，并探讨掩蔽释放。

研究主题

Second-language speech perception · Vowel perception · Formant-cue sensitivity · Masking release · Speech perception in adverse listening conditions

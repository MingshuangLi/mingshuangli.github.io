Frequency modulation detection in Mandarin Chinese-speaking amusics across modulation rate, carrier frequency, and stimulus duration

Mingshuang Li, Yun Nan, Wei Tang, Wenjing Wang, Chang Liu

引用信息

Li, M.*, Nan, Y., Tang, W., Wang, W., & Liu, C. Frequency modulation detection in Mandarin Chinese-speaking amusics across modulation rate, carrier frequency, and stimulus duration. JASA Express Letters, 6(7), 074403.

DOI: 10.1121/10.0044337

研究概述

本研究考察普通话先天性失乐症者的频率调制检测能力，并比较调制速率、载波频率和刺激时长等声学条件下的检测阈值。

研究主题

Amusia · Frequency modulation detection · Pitch processing

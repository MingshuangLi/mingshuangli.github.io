Sentence recognition and enhancement in noise: Effects of speech muffling, signal-to-noise ratio, and formant enhancement

Mingshuang Li, Chang Liu

引用信息

Li, M.*, & Liu, C. Sentence recognition and enhancement in noise: Effects of speech muffling, signal-to-noise ratio, and formant enhancement. JASA Express Letters, 6(7), 075204.

DOI: 10.1121/10.0044414

研究概述

本研究比较自然言语与 N95 口罩衰减言语在安静及四人语音噪声中的句子识别。结果表明，F2–F3 联合增强在不利聆听条件下的获益比单独 F2 增强更广泛，而安静条件下未见增强获益。

研究主题

Speech enhancement · Formant enhancement · F2 enhancement · F2–F3 enhancement · Speech intelligibility in noise · Face-mask-muffled speech
